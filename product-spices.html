Premium Cardamom & Cloves

Authentic Indian spices, processed to retain maximum flavor and essential oils.

Product Overview

As one of the leading exporters of Indian spices, we take pride in offering premium Green Cardamom and robust Cloves. Known for their intense aroma, natural color, and high volatile oil content, our spices are a staple in international culinary and pharmaceutical industries.

Our spices are sourced from the lush, tropical estates of Southern India. They undergo a rigorous multi-stage cleaning and grading process using advanced Sortex technology to ensure 99% purity.

Processing & Quality

  • Cleaning: Machine-cleaned to remove all stems, dust, and foreign particles.
  • Grading: Sorted by size (e.g., 6mm, 7mm, 8mm for Cardamom) and color density.
  • Storage: Stored in climate-controlled warehouses to prevent moisture loss.

Packaging Options

We offer flexible packaging solutions to meet client needs. Options include 10kg or 25kg PP bags, jute bags, or custom retail-ready pouches for private labeling.
