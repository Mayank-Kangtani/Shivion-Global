Premium Mangoes

The King of Fruits, exported globally with unparalleled freshness and taste.

Product Overview

Our Premium Mangoes are sourced directly from the finest orchards in India. Known globally for their rich, sweet flavor and vibrant color, our mangoes are carefully hand-picked at the optimal stage of maturity to ensure they ripen perfectly upon reaching your destination.

We primarily deal in two of the most sought-after varieties: Alphonso and Kesar. Every single fruit undergoes strict quality and size grading before packaging.

Varieties Available

  • Alphonso (Hapus): Renowned for its rich, creamy, tender texture and delicate, non-fibrous, juicy pulp.
  • Kesar: Famous for its distinctive golden color with a green tinge and exceptional sweetness.
  • Banganapalli: Large, oblique, and unblemished yellow skin with a firm, meaty texture.

Quality & Packaging

We utilize advanced corrugated box packaging that protects the fruit during air and sea transit. The packaging is well-ventilated and designed to maintain the optimal temperature required for fresh produce.
